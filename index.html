    A LITTLE LOCAL CONTEXT

    Before you set off.

    Simple answers to common questions.

    What money is used?

    Kazakhstan uses the tenge (KZT). Cards are common in cities; some cash can be useful in rural areas.

    What languages are spoken?

    Kazakh and Russian are widely spoken. A translation app can help when travelling outside major cities.

    What should I wear in the mountains?

    Bring layers, a windproof and rainproof shell, suitable footwear, water and a charged phone. Check local conditions first.

    Is this an AI assistant?

    These are prepared answers, not an AI service. They cannot replace current official advice.
